## Section 5: Types of Simulations to Run

### 1. Best/Realistic/Worst Case

```
Decision: Launch product next month
├─ Best: Launch on time, hits market window, competitors late → hit 10M revenue
├─ Realistic: Launch on time, market response slow → 2M revenue, 1 year break-even
└─ Worst: Delay 2 months, competitors launch, market saturated → 200k revenue
```

### 2. Sensitivity Analysis

```
"What changes most?"

Decision: Move to new city for job
├─ Salary +40% vs Rent +50% (net: same)
├─ Salary +40%, Rent +20%, Network -30% (net: good money, lonely)
└─ Salary +40%, Rent +50%, Career boost +60% (net: worth it 5 years)
```

### 3. Timeline Simulation

```
"When does it get hard?"

Decision: Go back to school
├─ Month 1: Exciting
├─ Month 4: Boring lectures, tired
├─ Month 8-10: Peak stress (exams + work)
├─ Month 12: "Almost there" momentum
├─ After: Credential valuable? Yes/No → whole decision changes
```

## Section 8: Limitations — Simulation is Not Perfect

### AI Twin Can Predict:

```
✓ Patterns based on your 12D
✓ High-probability outcomes
✓ Known risks
✓ Broad timeline (6mo, 1yr, 5yr)
```

### AI Twin Can't Predict:

```
✗ Black swan events (pandemic, family emergency)
✗ 100% accuracy (humans surprising)
✗ External factors (market crash, competitor moves)
✗ Your willpower (might quit earlier than predicted)
```

### Use Simulation As:

```
60% guide (not 100%)
+ 40% your judgment + values
= Better decision
```
