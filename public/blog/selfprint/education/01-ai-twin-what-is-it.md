## Section 7: Real Talk — Limitations

### AI Twin ≠ Crystal Ball

**It CAN:**
```
✅ Predict high-probability patterns
✅ Identify blind spots
✅ Guide decisions
✅ Accelerate self-awareness
```

**It CAN'T:**
```
❌ Account for 100% of variables
❌ Force you to change
❌ Replace human judgment
❌ Predict rare events
```

### Smart Use:

```
AI Twin = 1 input (70% weight)
+ Your intuition (20%)
+ Domain expert advice (10%)
= Good decision
```
