# Starting a Fitness Journey: Your Twin's Framework

Everyone starts fitness the same way: With big plans that fizzle.

January 1: "I'm going to work out 5 days a week!"  
January 15: Haven't gone once.

Your Twin knows why this pattern repeats—and how to break it.

## Why Fitness Goals Fail

People approach fitness like a destination: "I'll work out until I'm fit, then stop."

That's backwards. Fitness is a lifestyle, not a milestone.

**What fails:**
- Too much too fast (your body rebels)
- Trying to look like Instagram (that's genetics + lighting)
- Gym intimidation (you're surrounded by fit people)
- No community (working out alone is hard to sustain)

## The Twin Framework: Building Sustainable Fitness

### Step 1: Pick One Type of Exercise You Don't Hate

Not the most effective. Not the trendiest. One you could actually enjoy.

- Walking (free, no gear)
- Swimming (low impact, full body)
- Dancing (fun, burns calories)
- Strength training (builds confidence)
- Yoga (flexible, meditative)

If you hate it, you won't do it. Pick something tolerable.

### Step 2: Start Absurdly Small

"30 minutes, 3x per week" sounds reasonable.

It doesn't work. Start with:
- 10-15 minutes, 2x per week
- One walk around the block
- YouTube 10-minute workout

**This is on purpose.** You're building the habit, not the fitness.

### Step 3: Make It Inconvenient to Skip

- Lay out gym clothes the night before
- Schedule it like a meeting
- Tell someone you're going
- Join a class (paid commitment)

**Friction is your friend.** The more inconvenient to skip, the more likely you'll go.

### Step 4: Track Something

Not weight (fluctuates daily). Track:
- Workouts completed
- How you felt after
- Energy levels throughout day
- Sleep quality

These are real measures of fitness.

### Step 5: Build Community

Workout alone and it's boring. Workout with others and it's social.

- Friend who walks with you
- Gym class you attend
- Running group
- Online accountability partner

## The Timeline: What to Expect

**Weeks 1-4:**  
You're forcing it. Motivation is high but discipline carries you.

**Weeks 5-12:**  
Motivation fades. Habit is building. This is the hard part.

**Weeks 13+:**  
It starts feeling normal. You might actually want to go.

**3-6 months:**  
Fitness becomes part of your identity. You miss it if you skip.

Push through week 5-12. That's where most people quit.

## Key Takeaways

- Pick exercise you don't hate (not optimal, enjoyable)
- Start absurdly small (10-15 min, 2x/week)
- Make skipping inconvenient
- Track consistency, not results
- Build community for motivation

## How Your Twin Guides You

Your Twin helps you see that fitness is a marathon, not a sprint.

Start small. Build consistency. Let the results follow.
