# Choosing a Diet That Actually Fits Your Life

Every diet works for someone. The question was never "does this diet work" — it's "will this specific version of it survive contact with your actual week."

## The Question Everyone Skips

Most diet decisions get made by comparing macros, not by comparing lifestyles. But the diet that requires meal-prepping five dishes a week will lose to your actual Tuesday every single time, regardless of how good its research is.

Before comparing diets, compare your life: how much time do you have to cook, how often do you eat with other people, what's non-negotiable to you about food (culture, budget, social meals)?

## A Way Through It

Rank three diet approaches not by expected results, but by how many of your actual constraints they respect. The one that fits the most constraints — even if it's not the "optimal" one on paper — is the one that survives month three.

## Key Takeaways

- Adherence beats optimization; a good-enough diet you keep beats a perfect one you quit
- Audit your constraints before you audit the diets
- Month three is the real test, not week one
