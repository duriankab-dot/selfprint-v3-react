# Should You Start Therapy?

"I don't think it's bad enough for therapy" is one of the most common reasons people delay it by years. Therapy isn't a threshold you cross when things collapse — it's a tool available well before that.

## The Real Question

The useful question isn't "am I sick enough to deserve this." It's "is there a pattern in my life I keep hitting that I can't see my way through alone." That pattern doesn't need to be dramatic to be worth examining with someone trained to help you see it.

## A Way Through It

Try one practical filter: if you've had the same argument, the same stuck feeling, or the same regret more than three times in the last year, that's a pattern worth a professional's eyes — not because you're broken, but because outside perspective is genuinely hard to generate for yourself.

Starting doesn't commit you to years of it. A few sessions is enough to know if the fit and the format work for you.

## Key Takeaways

- Therapy doesn't require a crisis threshold to be worth trying
- Repeated patterns are a better signal than severity
- A trial of a few sessions is a low-risk way to find out

## How Your Twin Guides You

Your Twin can surface the patterns you might not notice yourself — the same kind of conflict recurring, the same decision regretted more than once. That's useful evidence to bring into the decision, whichever way you land on it.
