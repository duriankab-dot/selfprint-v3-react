# Recovering From Burnout, Not Just Surviving It

A long weekend off usually isn't recovery — it's a pause. Real burnout recovery is slower and less comfortable than most people expect, because it requires doing less before you're capable of doing more.

## Why the Weekend Doesn't Fix It

Burnout is a depleted reserve, not a single bad week. Two days off refills almost nothing when the underlying pace hasn't changed — you go back Monday and the deficit resumes exactly where it left off.

Real recovery means the pace itself has to change for a while, not just the location you're exhausted in.

## A Way Through It

Pick one obligation to genuinely drop for a month — not delegate, not do faster, actually drop. Notice what changes in your energy once that specific load is gone. That's more diagnostic than any amount of rest alone.

## Key Takeaways

- A weekend off is a pause, not a recovery
- Recovery requires a real reduction in ongoing load, not just a break from it
- One dropped obligation reveals more than generic rest advice

## How Your Twin Guides You

Your Twin can track your energy patterns across weeks, not just days — so it can tell you honestly whether a change actually helped, or whether you're just further into the same slope with better excuses.
