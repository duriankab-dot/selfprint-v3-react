# Marriage Proposal: Twin Wisdom on Forever

This is the biggest relationship decision. Marriage isn't a dating upgrade. It's a legal, financial, and emotional commitment that changes everything.

Your Twin knows: Romance is different from readiness.

## The Proposal Question vs. The Marriage Decision

**Proposal question:** "Will you marry me?" (emotional, in-the-moment)

**Real questions:**
- Can I live with this person for 30+ years?
- Are we aligned on life goals (kids, location, finances)?
- Can we resolve conflict healthily?
- Do I trust them fully?
- Am I marrying them or the idea of them?

## Before Proposing (or Saying Yes)

**Have these conversations:**

1. **Kids:** Do you both want them? How many? When?
2. **Money:** How do you handle finances? Merging accounts? Major purchases?
3. **Life path:** Where do you want to live? Career priorities? Travel?
4. **Conflict:** How do you fight? Can you resolve disagreements?
5. **Family:** How involved are parents/siblings? Boundary setting?
6. **Deal-breakers:** What couldn't you survive? (Infidelity? Abuse? Career demands?)

These aren't romantic. They're practical. And necessary.

## The Marriage Readiness Check

**You're ready if:**
- You know who you're marrying (not fantasy version)
- You've resolved major incompatibilities
- You can imagine them being your partner at 60
- You've survived at least one serious conflict together
- You trust them deeply

**You're not ready if:**
- You're avoiding a real conversation
- They're not who you thought (still discovering them)
- You're hoping marriage will fix problems
- You're afraid to say no
- You haven't lived together or know their daily habits

## What Marriage Actually Changes

**The good:**
- Legal protections and benefits
- Shared identity and partnership
- Long-term planning becomes easier
- You're choosing them repeatedly

**The hard:**
- You can't just leave (legal/financial hassle)
- Conflict is harder to avoid
- You're bound to their choices (debt, behavior)
- Romance fades; partnership is what remains
- You're committing to someone who will change

## Key Takeaways

- Marriage is different from dating
- Have the practical conversations first
- Know who you're actually marrying (not fantasy)
- Ready if: You know them, trust them, can live with them long-term
- Marriage is a commitment you choose repeatedly

## How Your Twin Guides You

Your Twin separates the romance of proposal from the reality of marriage.

Marriage is beautiful when it's chosen clearly. It's painful when it's chosen romantically.
