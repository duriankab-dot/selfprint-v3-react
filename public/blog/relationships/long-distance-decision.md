# Long Distance Love: Is It Worth It?

You got the job offer. It's across the country. Your partner is staying put.

Long distance is possible. The question: Is it worth the cost?

## The Long Distance Reality

Long distance requires:
- Trust (you're not seeing each other)
- Communication (intentional, frequent)
- Commitment (you have to want this)
- Money (visiting is expensive)
- Patience (months or years potentially)

It's not romantic. It's work.

## When Long Distance Can Work

**Works if:**
- You're both committed to an end date (1-2 years max)
- You visit regularly (monthly or every 6 weeks)
- You have solid communication habits
- You both believe this is temporary
- You trust each other deeply

**Doesn't work if:**
- You're trying to avoid a real conversation about the relationship
- One person wants it; one is reluctant
- You don't have money for visits
- You can't maintain communication
- There's no end date in sight

## The Twin Decision Matrix

```
Long distance decision:

STRENGTH OF RELATIONSHIP:
- Are we genuinely strong? (Score honestly)
- Do we fight fairly?
- Can we communicate well?

LOGISTICS:
- How far apart? (visiting difficulty)
- How often can we visit? (monthly or quarterly)
- Can we afford it?

END DATE:
- When will this end? (18 months? Never?)
- Are we both clear on the timeline?

ALTERNATIVES:
- One person move?
- Break up and revisit later?
- Open relationship?

DECISION:
Only pursue if: Strong relationship + regular visiting + clear end date + both committed
```

## The Hardest Part

The hardest part isn't missing each other. It's the *not knowing when you won't miss each other anymore.*

If there's no end date, long distance becomes slow deterioration.

## Key Takeaways

- Long distance is work, not romance
- Requires clear end date, regular visits, strong communication
- Only pursue if both people are genuinely committed
- No end date = relationship on borrowed time
- Sometimes breaking up is smarter than trying long distance
