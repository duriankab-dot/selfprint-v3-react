# Reconnecting with an Ex: Should You?

They texted. You were shocked. Maybe a little relieved.

Years have passed. You're not the same people. Maybe that's enough reason to try again.

Your Twin knows: Time changes things, but not always for the better.

## Why Exes Seem Appealing

**In reality:** You remember the good parts and forget the hard parts.

**The real question:** Why did you break up? Has that changed?

If you broke up because of:
- **Timing issues** → Time might have fixed it
- **Different life paths** → Paths might have aligned
- **Needed growth** → You might both have grown
- **Fundamental incompatibility** → That doesn't change

## The Reconnecting Decision

**Ask yourself:**

1. Why did we break up originally?
2. Has that reason actually changed?
3. Am I reaching out because I miss them, or because I'm lonely?
4. Can I forgive what happened?
5. Do I want them or do I want the version of us that existed?

**Only reconnect if:**
- The reason for breakup is genuinely resolved
- You're not running from current problems
- You can forgive past hurt
- You're both willing to start fresh

**Don't reconnect if:**
- You're lonely and they're convenient
- The same issues still exist
- You're testing whether they still want you
- You haven't actually processed the breakup

## Key Takeaways

- Exes are appealing partly because they're safe
- The reason you broke up matters
- Time alone doesn't fix incompatibility
- Reconnecting is valid if you've both genuinely changed
- Be honest about whether you're healing or escaping

## How Your Twin Guides You

Your Twin helps you see reconnecting clearly: Sometimes it works. Sometimes it's just repeating patterns.

The difference is whether the underlying issues actually changed—not whether you've changed your mind.
