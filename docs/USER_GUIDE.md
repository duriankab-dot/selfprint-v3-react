## 🎯 WHAT IS SELFPRINT?

Selfprint is your personal decision-making companion powered by AI. It helps you:

✅ **Record decisions** — Capture what you're deciding and why  
✅ **Track outcomes** — See how decisions turned out over time  
✅ **Learn patterns** — Discover your decision-making tendencies  
✅ **Improve clarity** — Get insights from your Twin (AI reflection)  
✅ **Explore expertise** — Organize decisions across 12 life contexts

---

## 🚀 GETTING STARTED

### Step 1: Sign Up
1. Go to https://www.selfprint.one
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your email
4. Use your biometric (fingerprint, face) or passkey to create account
5. Approve in browser security prompt

**Time:** 2 minutes

### Step 2: Meet Your Twin (Core Awakening)
1. Answer **5 questions** about yourself
   - "What brings you joy?"
   - "What challenges you?"
   - "What matters most?"
   - "How do you make decisions?"
   - "What's your vision for the future?"
2. Twin extracts your "essence" from responses
3. Your Twin is born! 🎉

**Time:** 5-10 minutes

### Step 3: Your First Decision
1. Click **"Log Decision"** button
2. Describe a decision you're facing
3. Optionally add choices you're considering
4. Click **"Save"**
5. Chat with your Twin about the decision

**Time:** 5 minutes

## 📋 CORE FEATURES

### 1️⃣ Decision Recording

**How to log a decision:**

1. Click **"New Decision"** button (top of page)
2. Enter the decision:
   ```
   Example: "Should I accept the job offer from TechCorp?"
   ```
3. Add context (optional):
   - Options you're considering
   - Pros/cons
   - Timeline (when you need to decide)
4. Click **"Save"**

**Why record decisions?**
- Captures what matters at that moment
- Enables tracking outcomes later
- Creates learning history over time

---

### 2️⃣ Decision Follow-Up

**30/90/180/365-Day Check-ins**

When you log a decision, you can schedule a follow-up:

1. **In 30 days:** Quick reflection on progress
2. **In 90 days:** Detailed outcome review
3. **In 180 days:** Long-term impact assessment
4. **In 365 days:** Annual reflection

**What happens:**
- You get a notification on the scheduled date
- You reflect: "How did this turn out?"
- Mark outcome: **Positive** | **Neutral** | **Negative**
- Twin learns from your patterns

---

### 3️⃣ Conversation with Your Twin

Your Twin is an AI reflection of your values and thinking patterns.

**Ask your Twin about:**
- ❓ "What should I prioritize here?"
- ❓ "How does this fit my values?"
- ❓ "What am I missing?"
- ❓ "What would future me think?"

**Twin uses context:**
- Your decision history
- Your past outcomes
- Your expressed values (from Core Awakening)
- Patterns in how you decide

**Tips:**
- Twin doesn't decide for you → guides your thinking
- More conversations = smarter Twin
- Twin remembers previous decisions

### 4️⃣ The 12 Worlds

Organize your decisions across 12 life contexts:

| World | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| **Career & Work** | Job offers, career changes, work projects |
| **Health & Wellness** | Fitness goals, medical decisions, diet |
| **Relationships & Family** | Dating, marriage, family conflicts |
| **Finance & Money** | Investments, major purchases, budgeting |
| **Personal Growth** | Learning, skills, self-improvement |
| **Creativity & Expression** | Art, writing, creative projects |
| **Travel & Adventure** | Trips, experiences, bucket list |
| **Home & Living** | Moving, renovating, lifestyle |
| **Spirituality & Meaning** | Purpose, beliefs, philosophy |
| **Social & Community** | Volunteering, groups, networking |
| **Entertainment & Hobbies** | Entertainment, fun, relaxation |
| **Legacy & Impact** | Long-term contribution, leaving impact |

**How to use Worlds:**
1. When logging a decision, select its World
2. Twin provides context-specific insights
3. Track your pattern per World (e.g., "Am I good at career decisions?")
4. Unlock badges as you explore each World

## 🎮 HOW TO USE EFFECTIVELY

### Session Structure

**Effective Selfprint session (15 min):**

1. **Prepare (2 min)**
   - What decision am I facing?
   - What context matters?

2. **Record (3 min)**
   - Log the decision
   - Describe options

3. **Converse (8 min)**
   - Chat with Twin
   - Explore perspectives
   - Ask clarifying questions

4. **Decide (2 min)**
   - Clarify your thinking
   - Commit to a choice or next step

---

### Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Record decisions as they happen
- Be honest with your Twin
- Reflect on outcomes
- Explore different World contexts
- Ask clarifying questions

❌ **DON'T:**
- Treat Twin as the decision-maker (you decide)
- Hide your real thinking
- Skip outcome tracking
- Log only big decisions (small ones matter too)
- Use for sensitive personal data (privacy mode available)

## 🔐 PRIVACY & SECURITY

**Your data is yours.**

- ✅ End-to-end encrypted conversations (in transit)
- ✅ Only you can see your decisions
- ✅ No ads or data selling
- ✅ Delete anytime (GDPR compliant)

**Privacy Options:**
- **Public:** Share specific decisions (future feature)
- **Private:** Only you see (default)
- **Blurred:** No Twin sees (for very personal decisions)

**Data you control:**
- Conversations
- Decisions
- Twin essence
- All can be exported or deleted

## 🎯 EXAMPLE WORKFLOWS

### Workflow 1: Career Decision

```
1. Log: "Should I take the job at TechCorp?"
   - World: Career & Work
   - Options: Accept / Decline / Negotiate

2. Chat with Twin:
   ❓ "How does this fit my growth goals?"
   ❓ "What about work-life balance?"
   ❓ "What would my future self want?"

3. Set follow-up: 90 days
```
